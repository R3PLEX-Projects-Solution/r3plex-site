About R3PLEX

Construction-domain knowledge, translated into practical digital tools.

R3PLEX Projects Solution is a Malaysia-based construction consultancy and technology venture focused on quantity surveying, cost management, procurement support, project coordination, digital reporting, construction documentation and practical construction technology.

Its flagship product, R3QBOT, is being developed as an AI-assisted quantity take-off and drawing-intelligence platform with professional review and auditability at the centre of the workflow.

How R3QBOT works

AI assists. Deterministic services calculate. The QS remains the authority.

The manual workflow remains a usable fallback whenever automated assistance is unavailable, unsuitable or rejected.

01 / INTERPRET

AI interpretation

Read drawing context, labels and likely relationships as assistance.

02 / GEOMETRY

Extract & validate

Derive or manually create measurable geometry and validate its structure.

03 / CALCULATE

Deterministic QS calculation

Calculate from approved geometry, confirmed scale and explicit treatment rules.

04 / REVIEW

Human review

Inspect, edit, accept, reject, classify and resolve warnings or assumptions.

05 / APPROVE

Professional approval

Authorised review converts draft measurement work into approved quantities.

06 / EXPORT

Structured export & audit

Prepare Excel / CSV output and retain decision, source and revision history.

Product principle: R3QBOT supports Quantity Surveyor judgement. It does not replace the responsible professional or present unreviewed AI output as an approved quantity.
Product roadmap

A narrow floor-area proof now. Broader quantity intelligence only after validation.

Floor area is the first commercial entry point, not the final product boundary.

Now / Phase 1

Floor Area

Complete the smallest end-to-end professionally controlled workflow.

  • Multi-page PDF workflow and scale confirmation
  • Manual polygon creation and editing
  • One-click Area for selected supported vector plans
  • Assumptions, deviations, warnings and review states
  • Professional approval, persistence and Excel / CSV export
Next / validated expansion

Structural Quantities

Expand only after customer demand, rules and benchmark evidence are established.

  • Beams
  • Slabs
  • Columns
Future

Connected Quantity Intelligence

Future modules remain subject to separate product, data, rules and pilot gates.

  • Walls, doors, windows and other architectural elements
  • Civil and M&E quantities
  • Cost knowledge, BOQ linkage and quotation review
  • Carbon, procurement and supplier evidence
  • BIM / ERP and other integrations

Current boundary: whole-page Auto Measure and automatic professional approval are not part of the immediate Phase 1 proof.
